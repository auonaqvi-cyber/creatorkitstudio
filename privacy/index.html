Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026. CreatorKit Studio is operated by AMALEEN GLOBAL LLC.

1. Overview & Operator Information

This Privacy Policy describes how CreatorKit Studio ("the Website", "we", "us", or "our") manages information when you visit our website at https://creatorkitstudio.pro. CreatorKit Studio is operated by AMALEEN GLOBAL LLC, a United States limited liability company.

We are committed to clear, factual transparency regarding how your content is processed, how analytics data is gathered, and how third-party advertising partners operate on our site.

2. User Content Processed by Tools

Many CreatorKit Studio tools perform their core processing directly in the user's browser, which means text, images, captions, and other content handled by those tools do not need to be uploaded to CreatorKit Studio servers.

When you input captions, scripts, text snippets, or upload images into browser-based tools such as our Line Break Fixer, SRT Caption Cleaner, Case Converter, Readability Checker, or Image Resizer, the transformations are executed locally via client-side JavaScript and HTML5 APIs. We do not store, view, log, or sell the content you process in these tools.

3. Website Analytics (Google Analytics / GA4)

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand general website traffic, popular tool usage, and overall platform performance. Analytics services may collect:

  • Device, operating system, and browser information.
  • Approximate location derived from IP address (such as country or region).
  • Pages visited, time spent on pages, and navigation paths.
  • Interaction events, button clicks, and tool switches.
  • Referral sources and search queries leading to the site.

This aggregate data helps us identify technical bugs, improve tool functionality, and optimize user experience across various screen sizes.

4. Advertising (Google AdSense)

To maintain free access to our tools, CreatorKit Studio displays advertisements served by Google AdSense and its advertising partners.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or by using opt-out mechanisms provided by the Network Advertising Initiative at aboutads.info.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

Our website utilizes local browser storage (localStorage) and cookies strictly for the following purposes:

  • Theme Preferences (creatorkit_theme): Storing your chosen visual display mode (Dark Mode or Light Mode) locally on your device.
  • Workspace Draft Caching (creatorkit_draft_text): Caching your active workspace text locally in your browser to prevent accidental text loss during page refreshes.
  • Workspace Category Filter (creatorkit_active_workspace): Remembering your selected tool category view (All, Blogger, or Creator mode).
  • Analytics & Advertising: Standard cookies placed by Google Analytics and Google AdSense as described in sections 3 and 4 above.

You can clear local browser data or configure your browser to block cookies at any time through your browser settings.

6. Third-Party Services and CDNs

To deliver, secure, and monitor our website, the following external third-party services and content delivery networks (CDNs) are utilized:

  • Google Analytics (googletagmanager.com): Aggregate, non-identifying traffic and usage analytics.
  • Google AdSense (pagead2.googlesyndication.com): Contextual advertising delivery.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com & fonts.gstatic.com): Delivering web typography (Inter and JetBrains Mono).
  • Cloudflare CDN (cdnjs.cloudflare.com): Delivering FontAwesome interface icons.
  • Tailwind CDN (cdn.tailwindcss.com): Delivering responsive interface styling framework.

These third-party providers handle data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

7. Data Security

We implement standard technical safeguards, including HTTPS encryption, to protect data transmitted between your browser and our website. Because client-side tools execute operations locally in your browser sandbox, your tool input remains within your local session. However, no internet transmission is entirely secure, and users should exercise normal discretion when handling sensitive data online.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

AMALEEN GLOBAL LLC reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our tools, legal requirements, or advertising practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
