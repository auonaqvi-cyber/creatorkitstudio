Delivering a polished video script without stuttering, forgetting key points, or constantly glancing down at notes is one of the biggest challenges creators and professionals face. An online teleprompter solves this by scrolling your script smoothly at eye level. In this guide, we cover script preparation, scrolling speeds, camera alignment, and practical techniques for natural delivery.

1. Preparing Your Script for Teleprompter Reading

Writing for spoken video is fundamentally different from writing for print. Follow these scripting guidelines:

  • Write How You Speak: Use short sentences, contractions (e.g., "we're" instead of "we are"), and natural conversational phrasing.
  • Spell Out Difficult Numbers & Acronyms: Write "twenty-four thousand" rather than "$24,000" so you don't hesitate while reading live.
  • Add Visual Pauses: Insert line breaks or brackets like [PAUSE] or [SMILE] to remind yourself to breathe and vary your vocal cadence.

2. Optimal Camera Alignment and Eye-Line Placement

The most common beginner mistake is placing the script far away from the camera lens, resulting in a noticeable off-camera stare. To achieve convincing direct eye contact:

  1. Position the Window Under the Lens: Keep the browser window narrow and align the scrolling text directly below your webcam.
  2. Increase Your Distance: Sit at least arm's length (3–4 feet) from your screen. The further away you sit, the smaller your eye movement angles appear to the viewer.
  3. Use Narrow Text Margins: Prevent your eyes from tracking side-to-side across wide monitor screens by keeping text centered in a narrow column.

4. Rehearsal & Delivery Tips

Run through your script at least once out loud before hitting record. If you stumble on a particular phrase during rehearsal, reword the sentence immediately in your script. Simplicity is key to confident delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I prevent my eyes from looking like they are reading off a screen?

Position your teleprompter window directly beneath or next to your webcam lens, reduce the text margin width so your eyes do not dart horizontally across the screen, and sit at least 3 to 4 feet back from the display.

What reading speed should I set on the teleprompter?

Most conversational video presentations range between 130 and 150 words per minute. Start with a slow scrolling speed (e.g. speed level 2 or 3) and adjust to your natural speaking cadence.

What is Teleprompter Mirror Mode used for?

Mirror mode flips text horizontally and vertically. It is designed for physical prompter rigs that use 70/30 beam-splitter glass mounted in front of a camera lens.
