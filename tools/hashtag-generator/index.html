How to Use This Tool

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Enter a topic keyword or niche phrase into the search box (e.g. 'digital marketing' or 'fitness nutrition').
  2. Click Generate to create platform-tailored hashtag sets for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and LinkedIn.
  3. Click 'Copy Set' next to any platform card, or click Copy Master Tag Set to copy all generated tags.
  4. Paste the tags into your social post caption or notes.

Practical Tips & Guidelines:

  • Hashtag recommendations vary by platform, content type, and audience. Focus on relevant, specific hashtags rather than adding large numbers of unrelated tags.
  • Mix broader topic tags with specific sub-niche tags that directly match what is shown in your post.
  • Avoid repetitive or irrelevant tags to keep your posts clear and focused for your followers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hashtags should I use on my posts?

Hashtag recommendations vary by platform and audience. It is generally best to focus on a targeted set of relevant, topic-specific hashtags rather than adding excessive tags.

Should hashtags be placed in the caption or comments?

Both placements allow your tags to be discovered. Placing them directly in the caption ensures they are immediately active when your post goes live.
