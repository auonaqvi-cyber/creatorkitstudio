SRT Caption Cleaner

Convert raw .SRT and WebVTT subtitle files into clean, readable text. Automatically strips timestamps (00:00:00,000 --> 00:00:00,000), sequence numbers, and formatting tags directly in your browser.

How to Use This Tool

Step-by-Step Instructions:

  1. Copy the raw `.srt` or `.vtt` text from your subtitle file or video editor.
  2. Paste the text into the left input area.
  3. The tool instantly strips timestamps, sequence counters, and styling tags, outputting clean text on the right.

Overview & User Guide: SRT Caption Cleaner

Converting Video Subtitles into Clean Transcripts

Video subtitle files exported from editing tools and platforms (such as YouTube, Premiere Pro, Final Cut, and CapCut) contain timestamp lines and numeric counters on every line. Reading or reusing raw subtitle text requires stripping these formatting headers.

CreatorKit Studio's SRT Caption Cleaner processes subtitle text locally in your browser without uploading your file to a server. It automatically removes timecodes and line numbers, leaving you with clean, continuous text suitable for articles, notes, and documentation.

Once your transcript is cleaned, you can generate search-ready titles and snippet descriptions using our SEO Meta & SERP Generator.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this tool remove any spoken dialogue?

No. The cleaner only removes timestamp headers and line counters, keeping all your spoken words intact.

Does it support both .SRT and .VTT files?

Yes. It handles both standard SubRip (.srt) and WebVTT (.vtt) timestamp formats.
